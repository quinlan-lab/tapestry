Figure 2. Recovering per-site parental haplotype labels in a nuclear family from unphased genotypes alone. A small simulated trio plus three kids is shown across three column-aligned panels; each column is one site, and informative sites are gated per panel (non-informative sites display as `.`). Cells are filled with the haplotype palette A/B (dad's hap1/hap2, blue/orange) and C/D (mom's hap1/hap2, green/pink); letters A/B/C/D refer to fixed parental haplotype slots, never to transmission status. (A) Ground truth. The four founder haplotypes (Dad A, Dad B, Mom C, Mom D) are drawn as colored 0/1 strings, followed by each kid's two phased homologs (paternal `p` row above maternal `m` row), with every cell colored by the founder haplotype it was inherited from. Kid3's paternal row switches from A to B mid-locus, marking a paternal recombination. (B) Maternal-side reconstruction from unphased data. Top: the unphased VCF genotypes for all five individuals, restricted to mom-informative sites (sites where mom is heterozygous and dad is homozygous). Middle-top: the raw maternally inherited allele extracted at each informative site (`fig5_1`). Middle-bottom: the backfill-and-swap-by-majority labeling step (`fig3_3`), which assigns each kid a C or D at every informative site. Bottom: a colored ground-truth track showing the maternal haplotype each kid actually inherited (C or D run-encoded), for direct comparison against the reconstructed labels above. (C) Paternal-side reconstruction. Same three tables as panel B, restricted to dad-informative sites and labelled A/B, plus a fourth `perform_flips_in_place` step that resolves residual orientation ambiguity by aligning consecutive informative sites — this step is a no-op on the maternal side and is therefore omitted from panel B. The bottom ground-truth track shows Kid3's paternal row switching from A (blue) to B (orange) at the recombination breakpoint, which the reconstruction recovers. Together, the three panels trace the pipeline from unphased GTs to per-site A/B/C/D haplotype labels and show that the recovered labels match ground truth, including across a recombination event.
