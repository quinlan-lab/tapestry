**Supporting Figure 1. Queries that discover the loci illustrated in Fig 1C, D from tapestry's haplotype-resolved BED.** Each snippet operates on the per-CpG (and, for panel B, per-SNV) tables illustrated in part in Fig 4C, D, with one column per parental haplotype (dad_A, dad_B, mom_C, mom_D), the kid's per-side phased values (kid_pat, kid_mat), and the founder-haplotype labels (pat_hap, mat_hap). **(A)** Homolog-specific LOM at an imprinted locus (c.f., Fig 1C and Fig 4C). The query selects the mom haplotype that was transmitted to the kid via mat_hap, then keeps CpGs where "kid_mat − mom_transmitted" is sharply negative on average across a small window — a maternal LOM, the canonical case at maternally methylated imprinting control regions. Mirroring the column choice (pat_hap / dad_A / dad_B) surfaces the paternal LOM case at paternally methylated loci; flipping the sign surfaces a homolog-specific gain on either side. **(B)** Compound genetic-epigenetic heterozygote (c.f., Fig 1D and Fig 4D). A three-step chain over two tables: (1) find methylation-outlier regions where exactly one parental hap is the outlier and the kid inherits that hap with roughly unchanged methylation; (2) find genotype-outlier SNVs with the analogous inheritance check; (3) inner-join the two and keep loci where the methylation-outlier and genotype-outlier come from different parents, i.e. the two hits are in trans.
