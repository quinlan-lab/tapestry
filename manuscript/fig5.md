**Figure 5. Covariation of methylation and founder haplotypes at known meQTLs in pedigree K1463 validates tapestry's founder-phasing method.** 
**(A)** Schematic of the meQTL test: the allele at a meQTL covaries with methylation on the haplotype hosting it. 
**(B)** rs9330298. Rosenski et al. 2025 ([doi:10.1038/s41467-025-57433-1](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-025-57433-1)) report that haplotypes carrying the C allele are unmethylated while those carrying the A allele are methylated. Tapestry-phased methylation across pedigree K1463, averaged over a small interval containing the SNP (chr1:153617695-153617953, hg38), covaries with the founder haplotypes accordingly. 
**(C)** rs12499263. Rosenski et al. 2025 ([doi:10.1101/2025.09.15.675351](https://doi.org/10.1101/2025.09.15.675351)) report low (high) methylation on haplotypes carrying the C (A) allele, the A allele disrupting DNA-binding of CTCF, a transcription factor required for protection from de novo methylation. Tapestry recovers this association in K1463 over an interval containing the SNP (chr4:184100296-184100698; hg38). 
**(D)** rs12636296. Rosenski et al. 2025 ([doi:10.1101/2025.09.15.675351](https://doi.org/10.1101/2025.09.15.675351)) report that allelic variation correlates with methylation at a CpG island at the ALG1L promoter (G allele unmethylated, T allele methylated), and hence with expression of the gene. Tapestry recovers this association in K1463 over an interval containing the CpG island and the SNP (chr3:125990135-125991474; hg38).
