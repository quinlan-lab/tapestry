**Figure 4. Tapestry phases methylation to founder haplotypes, allowing disease locus discovery.** **(A)** `hap1`'s allele sequence is compared with that of the two founder labels (`pat_B`, `mat_D`) at the heterozygous sites of a *hap-map block*; ticks mark per-site matches. The resulting mapping here is `hap1 → B`, `hap2 → D`. **(B)** Two read-backed phase blocks span one IBD segment; panel A maps to the left block, and an analogous allele-sequence match maps to the right. Top: per-CpG methylation levels on the `hap1` / `hap2` tracks. Bottom: the same methylation levels rebucketed per hap-map block onto founder-labelled tracks (`pat_B`, `mat_D`); the underlying biology — `B` hypermethylated, `D` hypomethylated — is correctly exposed. **(C)** Maternal LOM discovery against tapestry's per-CpG BED. Highlighted rows show a contiguous run where the kid's methylation level on the maternally transmitted haplotype falls below the mother's methylation level on the same haplotype. The associated query is illustrated in Supporting Figure 1A. **(D)** Compound genetic-epigenetic heterozygotes can be discovered from the per-CpG methylation BED (top) together with a table of founder-phased genotypes (bottom). Highlighted rows indicate a methylation outlier on one parental founder and a deleterious-variant outlier on the other. The associated query is illustrated in Supporting Figure 1B.
