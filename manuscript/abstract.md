PacBio HiFi sequencing reports DNA methylation status at every CpG in the human genome, but assigning each call to a specific parental homolog across a pedigree — a prerequisite for genome-wide discovery of homolog-specific methylation lesions — remains an unmet need. Such lesions are already established as disease-causing at a handful of known loci, across two classes: a homolog-specific loss of methylation at an imprinted locus (the leading cause of human imprinting disorders), and a compound genetic-epigenetic heterozygote, in which a coding loss-of-function on one parental homolog sits *in trans* with a methylation lesion silencing the other. Each known disorder is diagnosed today by a locus-targeted clinical workup while emerging methylation arrays cover only a fraction of CpGs and offer no parental phasing. We present Tapestry, a workflow that uses PacBio HiFi reads to phase methylation at all CpG sites in the human genome onto the founder haplotypes of a human pedigree (including the trio special case). With this output, both classes of homolog-specific lesion become discoverable genome-wide through short downstream queries. Tapestry is available at https://github.com/quinlan-lab/tapestry.
